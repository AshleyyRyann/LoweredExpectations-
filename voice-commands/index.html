Reverse Voice Commands

Dictate normally. Say a word backwards to give a command, like EDOMTIDE ("edit mode" reversed) to turn your voice into a keyboard.

Your browser doesn't support live speech recognition. Use Chrome or Edge, or test with the "Pretend I said" box below.
Dictation

The "Pretend I said" box runs text through the same engine as the microphone, so you can test without speaking.

Cheat sheet

Dictation mode: backwards words

Edit mode: say it like a keyboard

Add a number for repeats: "backspace five", "left three". Say exit to go back to dictation.

Spell mode

Say letters ("a", "bee") or NATO words ("alpha", "bravo"). "cap" capitalizes the next letter. "space", "backspace", exit.

Teach it your voice

If a trigger isn't being caught, pick it, hit Train, and say it once. The app remembers how your speech engine spells it.
